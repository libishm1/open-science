Medicine and clinical research

Systematic reviews, guideline and trial reading, and research writing for clinicians and clinical researchers, with patient data kept on your own computer.

Not for clinical decisions. This app and its skills are research and writing aids. They are not a medical device, have not been clinically validated, and must not be used to diagnose, treat, or decide on the care of a patient. A qualified professional must check every output.

Patient data and privacy

  • Use local models for anything identifiable. With Ollama, prompts and files stay on your machine. Cloud models are off by default; do not enable them for patient data.
  • De-identify first. Remove names, dates of birth, record numbers, addresses, and free-text identifiers before analysis, following your institution's rules.
  • Follow your approvals. Running locally does not replace ethics approval, data-protection agreements, or your institution's information-governance rules. The software is not certified for HIPAA, GDPR, or any other regulation.
  • Check the provider before you start. In each project with patient data, confirm the selected model provider is Ollama or another local server, not a cloud provider.

The counting rules in research-synthesis fit clinical reviews well: every exclusion has a count and a reason, comparison tables need one shared outcome measure, and a rapid AI-assisted search is reported as a rapid map, not an exhaustive search.

Example: screening a set of trial reports

  1. Export the search results to a CSV and add the columns duplicate, stage, decision, reason.
  2. Ask a local model to propose include or exclude for each abstract against your criteria, with the reason.
  3. Check every proposal yourself. The model's decision is a suggestion, not a screening result.
  4. Ask the app: "Count my screening results and draw the PRISMA flow." You get the counts and a flow diagram to put in the paper.

Reading guidelines and trial reports

  • Convert long guideline PDFs with pdf-to-markdown and read them section by section.
  • Ask for the page number behind every recommendation the agent quotes, and open the page to check it.
  • For translated material, tamil-translator (fork) gives a line-by-line bilingual draft. Medical translations still need review by a qualified translator.

Writing and figures

  • Plan and write manuscripts with academic-paper-strategist and academic-paper-composer, and typeset with latex-document.
  • Use the upstream figure-style rules for forest plots, flow diagrams and outcome figures.
  • Follow the reporting guideline for your study type (for example CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE) and keep its checklist next to the draft.

Which local model

For screening and extraction, the literature column of the model table shows how each local model did on extraction, screening, and refusing to invent a reference. Re-run the benchmark on your own computer before relying on a model.
