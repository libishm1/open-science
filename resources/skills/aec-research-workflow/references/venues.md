# Venues, templates and limits

Limits change. **Always read the current guide for authors** before cutting or formatting; the values below are what applied when last checked (2026) and are a starting point only.

## Journals

| Venue | Template | Rules met in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Elsevier *Energy and Buildings* | elsarticle (LaTeX) or Word | Abstract ≤ 250 words; around 10,000 words; references numbered by first appearance; funding and competing interests through the Elsevier declarations tool, CRediT in the manuscript; highlights with count and character limits |
| Elsevier *Building and Environment* | elsarticle | **Hard limit of 20 display items** (figures + tables); exceeding it can trigger desk rejection |
| Elsevier *Sustainable Cities and Society*, *Developments in the Built Environment*, *Results in Engineering* | elsarticle | Transfer offers are common after scope rejections; check the transfer journal's own limits |
| MDPI *Sustainability*, *Buildings*, *Energies* | MDPI Word template or `mdpi.cls` | Abstract around 200 words; ISO-4 journal abbreviations; required cover-letter statements; itemised Supplementary list; figures at ≥ 600 dpi as a zip; graphical abstract not identical to any figure; commas in five-digit numbers; all acronyms defined; a Word upload file and one response file per reviewer in the portal |
| Springer Nature construction journals | `sn-jnl` | Check the journal's reference style and declarations section |
| *Automation in Construction*, *IJAC* | Publisher templates | AEC and robotics IMRaD conventions apply (see [writing-style.md](writing-style.md)) |

## Conferences

| Venue | Rules met in practice |
|---|---|
| CAADRIA | Camera-ready Word template, 10-page limit, narrower text area than the review template (cut content when moving to it) |
| eCAADe | Workshops and papers; check the current call |
| CUMINCAD | Indexes CAAD conference papers (useful for literature search and lab listings) |

## Templates

Link to publisher templates; do not copy them into public repositories unless their licence allows it.

## AI-use disclosure

Publisher policies on generative AI change and differ by venue. For each submission: check the venue's current policy, flag it once to the authors, follow the authors' decision, and record who decided. Strip assistant and session identifiers from code packages unless the authors decide to disclose them.

## Before every submission

Measure word count, abstract length, display-item count, reference count and highlight limits **after the last change**, not before. Compile and read every page. See [manuscript/submission-compliance.md](manuscript/submission-compliance.md).
